## Mechanism for Column Movement: Trade-off Analysis

| Mechanism                | Pros                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Cons                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| :----------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Simple Sliding Mechanism | - Easiest to design and model<br>- Lowest possible part count                                                                                                                                        | - Prone to sticking and then jerking free<br>- Requires significant post-processing<br>- Highly dependent on 3D printer tolerances.<br>- Will wear down from sliding                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Rack and Pinion          | - Precise and reliable motion<br>- Durable with low wear                                                                                                                                             | - High complexity<br>- Bulky and hard to integrate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Ball Bearings in Track   | - Much smoother than a simple sliding mechanism (metal on plastic sliding)<br>- Low hardware cost                                                                                                    | - Assembly can be finicky and difficult<br>- Need a way to lock at extremities                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Magnets                  | - Provides tactile click when lock into place<br>- Won't wear out like physical detents                                                                                                              | - Will interfere with future implementations of hall effect switches<br>- Still need to solve smooth sliding issue                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Compliant Mechanism**  | - **Monolithic design:** The entire mechanism (guide, spring, and structure) can be a single 3D-printed part<br>- **Low Profile:** A single flexure is lower profile than multiple moving components | - **Fatigue failure:** The flexure may weaken and break after many actuation cycles<br>- **Design complexity:** Requires iterative design and testing to perfect the feel and ensure durability<br>- **Material dependent:** Performance relies heavily on the chosen filament (e.g., ABS is better suited than brittle PLA) and print settings |
## Summary for Choosing a Compliant Mechanism

%% The compliant mechanism option was chosen because it aligns with the core goals of this project, and presents the most innovative and holistic solution that is the signature of the 3D FDM printing, the manufacturing process of this keyboard.
 %%
The main goal of this keyboard is to keep it fully 3D printable and modular, and a compliant mechanism fits that goal perfectly. For FDM printing, compliant mechanisms are essentially DFM in its purest form. They replace what would normally be bearings, fasteners, and linkages with a single, elegant, holistic part.

The main downside is fatigue failure. I’m addressing it through a few strategies:
- Material choice: favoring ABS over more brittle materials like standard PLA
- Iteration: refining the geometry to spread stress more evenly across the flexure
- DFM: aligning flexures with layer lines to take advantage of FDM’s strengths while avoiding its weak directions
- Modularity: since the mechanism is a standalone part, any long-term failure can be fixed by reprinting just that component, not the entire keyboard
